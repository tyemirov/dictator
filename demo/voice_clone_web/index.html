Dictator Example

Read classic passages in your own cloned voice

Record a short sample and the backend bridge will send it to a running Dictator gRPC service, then return a WAV file of the passage you choose read back in your voice. Read the sample twice if you want a steadier clone.

Step 1

Record your voice sample

Press the red button once to start. When it becomes a microphone, you are live. Press it again to stop.

Click the record button, read the sample slowly and clearly, then click the microphone to stop.

I grew up near a busy street, so even now I sleep best with a little noise in the distance. My friends know I speak quickly when I am excited, slow down when I am serious, and laugh before I finish the punch line. On cold mornings I want strong coffee, warm light, and ten quiet minutes to think. If you know me well, you can hear the difference between my polite voice, my tired voice, and the one I use when I am truly delighted.
Step 2

Choose What To Render

The backend bridge already has the Dictator auth token. Qwen3-TTS will use your full recorded sample plus the fixed transcript you just read.

Engine Qwen3-TTS is the only voice cloning engine in this demo.
